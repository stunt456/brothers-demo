How we estimate: Boneless yield is based on published deer studies (~43–50% of field-dressed weight; Ohio DNR ~48.5%, University of Wyoming Extension ~48%). “No cuts” treats that boneless meat as trim. “Standard cuts” subtracts a typical share kept as chops, steaks, knuckle roasts, and tenderloins. Shot shoulders/hinds use Wyoming Extension’s packaged-yield losses (−3.1 and −1.9 percentage points of dressed weight). Sources are listed in the project plan. Shop floor weights always win.
